    Welcome to Urban Balcony

    We specialize in transforming small urban balconies and rooftops into green and relaxing spaces. With years of experience in urban gardening and landscape design, we create customized solutions that bring nature into your urban home. Whether you're a homeowner looking to upgrade your living space, a young family wanting to create a green and educational environment for children, or luxury apartment residents seeking the perfect living experience - we're here to make your vision come true.

    Our services include comprehensive planning, selection of plants adapted to urban climate conditions, smart irrigation systems, and ongoing maintenance. We believe that every balcony, even the smallest one, can become a green sanctuary that improves quality of life and connects us to nature.

    Our Leading Services

    Balcony Planning and Design

    Comprehensive planning and establishment of a small balcony garden tailored to your needs, including plant selection, furniture arrangement, and irrigation systems.

    Customized Plant Selection

    Expertise in selecting plants suitable for urban climate conditions, sun exposure levels, and your maintenance requirements.

    Smart Irrigation Systems

    Installation of automatic and smart irrigation systems that save time and water, ensuring your plants receive the exact amount of water needed.
