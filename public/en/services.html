    • Our Professional Services

    Comprehensive solutions for urban balcony design and gardening - from planning to ongoing maintenance

    Urban Balcony Design and Planning

    Our comprehensive service begins with a personal consultation meeting where we learn about your needs, preferences, and budget. We conduct a detailed survey of the balcony or rooftop, examining sun exposure conditions, wind directions, and weight and space limitations. We then create a detailed design plan including diagrams, 3D visualizations, and a recommended plant list.

    Our planning takes into account all aspects - from selecting resilient plants suitable for urban climate conditions, through furniture arrangement and decorative elements, to installing irrigation and lighting systems. We specialize in creating gardens on small balconies and know how to maximize every centimeter while maintaining a sense of space and airiness.

    Our goal is to create an urban jungle balcony that reflects your personality and provides you with a green and relaxing space you can enjoy throughout the year.

    Plant Selection and Installation

    Selecting the right plants is critical to project success. We specialize in plants particularly suited to urban conditions - resistant to air pollution, adapting well to varying exposure conditions, and requiring reasonable maintenance. Our variety includes green ornamental plants, seasonal flowers, herbs, and edible plants.

    We work with leading suppliers and select high-quality, healthy, and strong plants. Each plant comes with detailed care instructions and recommendations for watering, fertilizing, and pruning. We also offer seasonal plant replacement services to keep your balcony colorful and vibrant throughout the year.

    Additionally, we specialize in selecting designed planters and growing containers that complement the design style and ensure optimal growing conditions for the plants.

    Smart Irrigation Systems

    A proper irrigation system is the key to maintaining your balcony garden. We install advanced automatic irrigation systems specially adapted to the urban environment. Our systems include smart timers that can be programmed according to a precise schedule, moisture sensors that check soil moisture levels, and efficient drip systems that save water.

    We offer a variety of solutions - from simple irrigation systems with basic timers, through sophisticated drip systems with multiple separate irrigation zones, to smart systems that can be controlled remotely via smartphone app. Each system is tailored to your specific balcony needs and the types of plants you've chosen.

    Installing an automatic irrigation system not only saves you valuable time, but also ensures that plants receive the exact amount of water they need, leading to healthier plants and water conservation.

    Ongoing Maintenance

    A balcony garden requires regular maintenance to stay beautiful and healthy. We offer professional maintenance services tailored to your needs - from monthly visits to full weekly service. Our experienced team takes care of all the details: plant pruning and cleaning, regular fertilizing, irrigation system checks, pest and disease treatment, and plant replacement as needed.

    We understand that every balcony is unique and requires customized care. Therefore, we create a personal maintenance plan based on your plant types, season, and desired maintenance level. Our team arrives at regular times, equipped with all necessary tools and materials, and performs the work efficiently and professionally.

    In addition to ongoing maintenance, we also offer emergency services for urgent problems such as pest outbreaks or plant damage due to severe weather conditions.

    Ecological and Sustainability Consulting

    We believe in responsible and sustainable gardening. Our services include comprehensive consulting on ecological gardening methods, use of local and resilient plants, water conservation, and use of organic fertilizers and environmentally friendly pest control materials. We teach our clients how to maintain their balcony garden sustainably and contribute to improving air quality and the urban environment.

    We also offer innovative solutions such as green walls, balcony composting systems, and organic edible plant cultivation. Our goal is to create balconies that are not only beautiful, but also contribute to the environment and urban quality of life.

    Luxury Rooftop and Balcony Design

    For clients seeking a luxury living experience, we offer advanced design services for private rooftops and balconies. Our projects include comprehensive landscape planning, installation of synthetic or natural grass, designed lighting systems, luxurious seating areas, and even water features like small fountains or ornamental pools.

    We work with leading designers and use the highest quality materials to create unique and impressive spaces. Each project is carefully planned taking into account structural limitations, building regulations, and safety requirements. The result is a balcony or rooftop that serves as a true extension of the living space and adds significant value to the property.
